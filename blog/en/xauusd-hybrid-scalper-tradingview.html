If you trade on TradingView and are looking for a free strategy for the gold market, "XAUUSD Hybrid Scalper v2" is a great option. It is written in Pine Script version 6 and is completely free (MPL 2.0 license).

What Is This Strategy?

XAUUSD Hybrid Scalper v2 is a hybrid strategy that runs two independent trading systems together:

  • System 1 — SMA Crossover: fast (14) and slow (28) SMA crossover — classic Long/Short signals.
  • System 2 — Hybrid Scalper: trend (EMA20 above EMA50), RSI crossover of level 50, strong candle and a session filter — more precise Buy/Sell signals.

How It Works

Long entry (SMA system): fast SMA crosses above the slow SMA.

Short entry (SMA system): fast SMA crosses below the slow SMA.

Buy (hybrid): EMA20 > EMA50 + RSI crosses above 50 + strong bullish candle + inside the session.

Sell (hybrid): EMA20 < EMA50 + RSI crosses below 50 + strong bearish candle + inside the session.

Exit: Stop Loss 1.5 × ATR and Take Profit 2.0 × ATR (ATR period: 14).

How to Add It to TradingView

  1. Download the Pine Script file from the link below.
  2. Open an XAUUSD chart in TradingView.
