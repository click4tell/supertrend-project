What is a SuperTrend Expert Advisor and How Does It Work?

Published: August 17, 2026 | Category: Products

SuperTrend is one of the most popular trend-following indicators in MetaTrader. Now imagine that same indicator turned into a trading robot that trades without your involvement. This article explains in simple terms what the SuperTrend Expert Advisor is and how it works.

What is the SuperTrend Indicator?

SuperTrend is a technical indicator that draws a line on the chart. When the market is bullish the line is below price and green; when bearish, the line is above price and red. A colour change is a trend reversal signal. Its main parameters are Period (usually 10) and Multiplier (usually 3).

What is the SuperTrend Expert Advisor?

The SuperTrend EA is the SuperTrend strategy converted into code that runs automatically. Instead of watching the line colour and placing trades manually, the robot does it for you.

How Does the SuperTrend EA Work?

  1. It calculates the SuperTrend line on the chart.
  2. When the line turns green → buy signal (Buy order opened).
  3. When the line turns red → sell signal (Sell order opened).
  4. It manages Stop Loss and Take Profit based on your settings.

This style is excellent for trending markets, but in range-bound (sideways) markets it can produce repeated false signals.

Advantages

  • Simple and easy-to-understand strategy.
  • Suitable for following strong trends in currency pairs and gold.
  • Fully automated, emotion-free execution.
  • Adjustable for different timeframes (H1, H4 and D1).

Risks and Disadvantages

In non-trending markets, SuperTrend can generate consecutive losses. No robot offers guaranteed profits, and bad settings (very small period or multiplier) create signal noise. Always test on a demo account first.

Recommended Settings

  • Timeframe: H1 or H4
  • Period: 10
  • Multiplier: 3
  • Risk management: 1–2% of balance per trade maximum
