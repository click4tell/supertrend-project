Forex Fury Robot Review (Forex Fury)

Published: August 17, 2026 | Category: Review

Forex Fury is one of the best-known Forex trading robots and has been on the market for years. In this article we review its pros, cons and performance impartially so you can make an informed decision.

What is Forex Fury?

Forex Fury is an Expert Advisor for MetaTrader 4 and 5 that trades automatically on major currency pairs. It became popular thanks to its ease of use and published backtest reports.

Pros

  • Fully automated trading with no need for constant monitoring.
  • Long track record and backtest documentation.
  • Simple installation and setup.
  • Suitable for mid-term timeframes.

Cons and Notes

  • In highly volatile markets it may experience large drawdowns.
  • Results are sensitive to broker, spread and execution speed.
  • Like all robots, it offers no guaranteed profits.
