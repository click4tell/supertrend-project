Forex Fury Robot Review (v13.5)

Published: August 17, 2026 | Updated: August 19, 2026 | Category: Review

Forex Fury Professional is a MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor that trades fully automatically in range markets. The latest version of this robot is 13.5. In this article we review its performance, capital protection features and backtest results.

What is Forex Fury?

Forex Fury is a range scalper: when the market has no trend, it uses pin bar + ADX filtering and Bollinger Band mean reversion to open short-term trades. Its best instrument is EURUSD on M15. Version 13.5 was redesigned specifically for broker compatibility and capital protection.

Version 13.5 Features

  • Fully automated trading (pin bar + ADX + Bollinger Bands + ATR).
  • Three-layer capital protection: daily loss limit (10%), 25% peak-equity drawdown stop, and automatic trading resume after 30 days.
  • Automatic margin protection: if the broker lowers leverage in critical times (e.g., 500 → 200), the robot shrinks the lot automatically instead of stopping.
  • Full broker compatibility: auto filling-mode detection, volume/price normalization, correct pip calculation.
  • Dedicated activation system (real accounts only — demo & backtest free).

Pros

  • Fully automated trading with no need for constant monitoring.
  • Automatic capital protection and no margin-call risk.
  • Simple installation and setup.
  • Real-tick backtest documentation.

Cons and Notes

  • Designed for range markets only; it does not trade strong trends.
  • Scalping is inherently risky and results depend on the broker, spread and execution speed.
  • Like all robots, there is no guaranteed profit.
