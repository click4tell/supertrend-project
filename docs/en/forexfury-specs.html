Forex Fury Professional EA — v13.5.0

Range scalper with a three-layer capital protection system for MetaTrader 5 (optimized: EURUSD on M15)

Overview

Forex Fury Professional EA (v13.5.0) is a range scalper: when the market is in a range (no trend), it uses pin bar + ADX detection and Bollinger Band mean reversion to open short-term trades. Entries/exits are controlled by Bollinger Bands, ATR and dynamic money management. Version 13.5 was fully redesigned from the earlier builds (up to v12) to be compatible with any broker and to protect capital against margin calls.

How It Works (from the real source code)

Step 1 — Pin bar detection: the robot checks the previous candle; bullish/bearish pin bar with a long shadow and small body (body < 30% of the range).
Step 2 — Range detection: it checks the last 12 bars; if ADX (period 20) is below 36 on all of them → the market is in a range.
Step 3 — Volume filter: trading volume must be above the average (MA20 × 1.6) to confirm the entry.
Step 4 — Mean reversion: price approaches the Bollinger Band (period 11, deviation 1.4) and the robot trades the return to the mean.
Step 5 — SL / TP: based on ATR (period 19): Stop Loss 6 × ATR and Take Profit 11 × ATR.
Step 6 — Dynamic management: Breakeven after +1 pip, trailing stop (start 1.0, step 0.9 pips — the profitable v12 profile with correct pip units).
Step 7 — Protection system: daily loss limit 10% (pause until the next day) + 25% peak-equity drawdown (emergency stop) + auto-resume after 30 days + margin control at 40%.
Step 8 — Extra filters: spread (max 1.5 pips + dynamic 1.5× average), trading hours 5–22 GMT, news filter (30 minutes around 8:30) and max 5 trades per day.
