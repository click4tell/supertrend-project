Forex Fury Professional EA — v10.0.0

Range scalper with trend filter for MetaTrader 5 (optimized: EURUSD on M15)

Overview

Forex Fury Professional EA (v10.0.0) is a range scalper: when the market is in a range (no trend), it trades price reversion to the mean. Range detection uses ADX, while entries/exits are controlled by Bollinger Bands, ATR and dynamic money management.

How It Works (from the real source code)

Step 1 — Range detection: the robot checks the last 12 bars; if ADX (period 20) is below 36 on all of them → the market is in a range.
Step 2 — Volume filter: trading volume must be above the average (MA20 × 1.6) to confirm the entry.
Step 3 — Mean reversion: price approaches the Bollinger Band (period 11, deviation 1.4) and the robot trades the return to the mean.
Step 4 — SL / TP: based on ATR (period 19): Stop Loss 6 × ATR and Take Profit 11 × ATR.
Step 5 — Dynamic management: Breakeven after +10 pips, trailing stop (start 10, step 9 pips), max drawdown 5%.
Step 6 — Extra filters: spread (max 15 pips + dynamic 1.5× average), leverage 500, trading hours 5–22 GMT, news filter (avoid 8:30 for 2 hours) and max 54 trades per day.
