How It Works

1. SuperTrend Calculation

HL2 = (High + Low) / 2

Upper Band = HL2 + (Multiplier × ATR)

Lower Band = HL2 − (Multiplier × ATR)

✅ Close > Upper Band → uptrend

✅ Close < Lower Band → downtrend

2. Signal Generation

  • BUY signal: at least 5 bullish timeframes + all filters PASS
  • SELL signal: at least 5 bearish timeframes + all filters PASS
  • Sound alert on signal change

3. Trade Execution

  • Auto Direct: instant entry at Ask/Bid + fixed SL/TP
  • Auto Pending:
    • Buy Limit: Ask − (ATR × Multiplier)
    • Sell Limit: Bid + (ATR × Multiplier)
    • Pullback Strategy
